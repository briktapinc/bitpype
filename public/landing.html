Win clients.

The front of the agency: finding businesses, proving value, and turning replies into customers.

  • Lead pipeline, scored

    Every prospect in one pipeline — researched facts with sources, 0–100 scoring and qualification, tags, groups and CSV import.

  • LeadScout capture

    Form submissions, bookings and social captures become deduplicated contacts automatically. One email, one lead — never a duplicate.

  • Claim pages & follow-up

    Send a prospect a page to claim their site. When they tap “I'm interested”, the follow-up email goes out on its own.

  • Outreach campaigns

    Email and SMS campaigns run from the same lead list, with a suppression list respected across sends.

  • Rent-to-Lead sites

    Rent a finished site to one exclusive local business and charge exactly once per qualified event — form captures and counted calls.

  • Promo lead forms

    A public intake form for your own ads, with captcha protection and brochure uploads that feed the design brief.

Build & launch sites.

What your clients actually receive: a real website, on their domain, with working parts — not a template and a prayer.

  • AI-designed, one at a time

    Design jobs build a unique site around each business's real facts, then automated QA reviews screenshots before anything is delivered.

  • 20 native modules

    Blog, shop, courses, gallery, directory, FAQ, testimonials, team, events, bookings, chatbot, offers, menus, careers, video, instant estimate, social feed, call tracking, memberships and Google Reviews.

  • Bookings that book

    Services and weekly hours drive a real slot picker, so customers choose an actually-open time — not a “preferred date” text box.

  • Call tracking

    A tracked number on the site forwards to the client. Calls are counted, recorded and transcribed, and voicemail reaches the owner by email.

  • Domains, handled

    Search, buy and connect domains inside the app. DNS is pointed automatically and the site publishes to Cloudflare Pages.

  • SEO foundations

    Every export ships a sitemap, robots file, a real 404 page, canonical URLs, and LocalBusiness and FAQ schema built from verified facts.

Run the business.

The back office your clients never see — and the reason the agency stays manageable as it grows.

  • Client portal

    Clients preview and edit their site, read form submissions, manage bookings and members, and see their plan — without calling you.

  • Plans & Stripe billing

    Free, Standard, Pro and Premium plans on Stripe subscriptions. Edit an amount in admin and the app creates and switches the Stripe price for you.

  • Finance dashboard

    Domain costs, AI spend and manual expenses in one ledger with CSV export — what the agency actually spends, by period.

  • Forms & memberships

    A form builder for every site, plus memberships with gated pages and paid tiers that settle to the site owner's own Stripe account.

  • Google Business Profiles

    Prepare and validate profile updates for clients from the portal, with every change set tracked per business.

  • Security posture

    Turnstile on public forms, per-IP rate limits that survive restarts, staff MFA, and role-gated destructive actions.

Powered by AI.

This is the part a normal website builder cannot copy: the platform is built to be driven by AI agents, not just clicked by humans.

  • An MCP API with 200+ tools

    Leads, design, modules, domains, publishing and billing are exposed as tools, so an AI agent can run the same workflows your team does.

  • Agent-native design jobs

    Briefs wait in a queue an AI designer picks up: deliver the site, take the automated QA notes, redesign, improve — a real production loop.

  • AI site chatbot

    Client sites can carry a chatbot that answers visitors and routes the conversation into the client's portal.

  • Research & scoring built in

    Lead research, scoring and outreach drafting are model-assisted steps inside the pipeline — with facts kept separate from copy.

  • Call transcription

    Recorded calls are transcribed automatically, so a client's phone leads are readable next to their form leads.

  • The same desk, every client

    Because agents and staff share one toolset, the work is consistent across every site your agency runs.

Questions.

Is this the same software BitPype uses for its own clients?

Yes — this page is about the platform itself. The client websites BitPype builds and manages run on this software, through the same pipeline described above.

Do I need to be a developer?

The day-to-day work happens in admin screens, the client portal, and AI agents working through the MCP tools. The design pipeline is agent-run: you bring the clients and the judgment, the software does the building.

What happens when I register interest?

Your name, email and phone land in our own lead pipeline — the very system this page describes — and the team gets in touch. Access and pricing are being set with the first group of operators, which is why the form comes first.

Can my clients log in and manage their own sites?

Yes. Every client site comes with the portal: preview and edits, form submissions, bookings, members and plan details, on a computer or a phone.
