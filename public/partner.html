The terms, in plain words.

  • Free to join. There is no partner license fee and no monthly partner charge. You earn commission; that is the whole model.

Questions.

Is partnering really free?

Yes. There is no fee to apply, no license to buy and no monthly partner charge. BitPype earns its share from the subscriptions your customers pay; you earn 30% of those payments. The only money that ever moves is customer subscriptions and your payouts.

Why is there no public signup?

Because we don't want tourists. Partner accounts are created by our team after we review an application — a partner represents BitPype to their customers, so we keep the door narrow and the standard high.

When exactly do I get paid?

Your balance grows with every customer payment (30%, credited on payment). Once the balance reaches $500 you are payout-eligible, and payouts are made monthly. Your earnings dashboard shows the per-payment history and every payout we have recorded.

What if a customer cancels or gets a refund?

Commission is earned on payments that stay collected. If a payment is refunded or disputed, that payment's commission reverses out of your balance — the per-payment history in your dashboard shows it.

Can I set my own prices?

Partners sell the standard BitPype plans at the standard prices — it keeps the offer simple and the same for every customer. If you want your own pricing and to keep 100%, look at Solo: your own instance of the whole platform, licensed monthly.

Do I need to be technical?

No. The platform does the heavy lifting — AI-assisted design, bookings, calls, forms and follow-up built in — and we walk you through your workspace before you start. Your skill is your audience and looking after your customers.
