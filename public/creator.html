Questions.

Is Solo a franchise?

No. Solo is a license to run your own instance of the BitPype platform as your own business — your brand, your domain, your prices, your customers, your Stripe account. We install it, train you, and hand over full admin.

Do I need an audience of business owners?

Not only business owners. The audiences that fit best are full of people building something — a trade, a salon, a food truck, a coaching offer, a side business, an idea they keep posting about. If your followers regularly ask how to get started or get more customers, that is the signal.

Do I set my own prices?

Yes. The $99/month in the examples above is only an example. Your instance carries your plans at your prices, and payments settle to your own Stripe account.

What happens when I apply?

Your application lands in our own lead pipeline — the same system this page describes — and we review it personally. If your audience fits, we will walk you through the platform, the deployment and your first 90 days before anything is decided.
