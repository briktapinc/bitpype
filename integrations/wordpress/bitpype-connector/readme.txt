Connect this WordPress site to your BitPype account and put your BitPype forms on any page.

== Description ==

The BitPype Connector is a free plugin. The copy you installed was generated for you when you downloaded it from your BitPype portal, with a one-time registration code inside: on activation it connects this site to your BitPype workspace automatically — no keys to copy, no license to activate.

Once connected you can place any of your BitPype forms on any page or post with the "BitPype Form" block or the [bitpype_form] shortcode. Submissions arrive in your BitPype portal under LeadScout, marked with the WordPress page they came from.

Version 2 adds design editing. In your BitPype portal's WordPress tab, the design chat can propose changes to your site's global look (colors, fonts) and to BitPype sections (banners, heroes, calls to action placed with the "BitPype Section" block or the [bitpype_zone] shortcode). Nothing is ever applied without you: you preview the proposal on your real site, approve or reject it in the portal, and the plugin snapshots your current design before applying — so any applied change can be undone from the portal. Page content, theme files and other plugins are never touched.

== Installation ==

1. In your BitPype portal, open the WordPress tab and click "Download the plugin" (each download connects one WordPress site).
2. In WordPress: Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin, choose the downloaded zip, then Install Now and Activate.
3. The plugin connects itself. Check Settings → BitPype to see your forms and their shortcodes.
4. Add a form to a page with the BitPype Form block, or paste a shortcode such as [bitpype_form id="3"].

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= What happens if I deactivate the plugin? =
BitPype shows this site as Disconnected and your forms stop appearing. Reactivating reconnects the same site — your connection is saved. Submissions you already received stay in your BitPype portal either way.

= What happens if I delete the plugin? =
Its settings on this site are wiped, so this downloaded copy cannot connect again (its registration code was already used). Just download a fresh copy from your BitPype portal. After uninstalling, remember to remove the [bitpype_form] and [bitpype_zone] shortcodes from your pages — they stop showing once the plugin is gone.

= Can the design chat change my site by itself? =
No. It can only prepare proposals. You preview each proposal on your real site and approve or reject it in your BitPype portal; the plugin applies only what you approved, snapshots the previous design first, and any applied change can be undone from the portal.

= Is there a license key? =
No. The plugin is free. Your BitPype subscription controls your BitPype features; the plugin is just the bridge.
